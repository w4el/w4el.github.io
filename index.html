Skin lesion classification

MSc dissertation, Queen Mary University of London, 2026

Recent papers report that cross-attention is the best way to combine skin lesion images with patient metadata. I tested that claim. Five models shared one EfficientNet-B0 backbone, one training setup and a split grouped by lesion, so only the fusion method changed.

PyTorch, EfficientNet-B0, scikit-learn, SHAP, Grad-CAM, McNemar's test, bootstrap confidence intervals

Code on GitHub

  • Cross-attention did not beat simple concatenation on macro-F1.
  • A random split put 35% of test images on lesions already seen in training. The inflated scores reversed the ranking of the two best models.
  • Gated fusion caught the most melanomas, with a sensitivity of 0.809.
  • The metric you report can pick the winner. Accuracy, macro-F1 and melanoma sensitivity ranked the models differently.
  • I traced a mixed-precision bug that logged AUROC as zero, and recomputed every metric from saved checkpoints.

Robotic pick-and-place with YOLOv8-OBB

BSc final year project, University of Greenwich, 2025

A simulated robot arm that finds parts with a camera, works out how they are turned, and picks them up.

  • Built a Python pipeline that renders CAD models and works out exact oriented-box labels from the camera geometry, so about 1,000 training images needed no hand labelling.
  • Fine-tuned YOLOv8-OBB and connected it to MoveIt2 through ROS 2 nodes. The UR5 arm succeeded in 82% of 100 simulated trials.

Python, YOLOv8-OBB, ROS 2, MoveIt2, Gazebo

Companies knowledge graph

Data Semantics coursework, Queen Mary University of London, 2026

A Python pipeline that fills an OWL 2 ontology with company data from Wikidata and DBpedia.

  • Linked 41 of 42 companies across both sources.
  • Queried the graph with SPARQL and added seven SWRL rules, run with Drools, that derive new facts such as profit per employee.
  • Diagnosed a reasoning error that merged New York with London into one city.

Python, rdflib, OWL 2, SPARQL, SWRL, Protégé
